🔧 STM32F103 ESC (Sensorless BLDC Controller)

Final Architecture – Reverse Engineered (v1.0 Archive)

📌 Overview

This project documents a fully reverse-engineered BLDC ESC based on STM32F103 with a complete hardware stack for sensorless control.

The controller supports:

6-PWM motor control (TIM1 complementary outputs)

Phase current sensing (3x shunt)

Back-EMF sensing (3x ADC)

Battery voltage monitoring

Temperature measurement (NTC)

UART debugging & telemetry

External control inputs (throttle + brake)

🔁 Commutation (6-Step)
Step	High	Low	Floating
0	A+	B-	C

⚠️ Driver Characteristics (FD2103S)

HIN → active HIGH

LIN → active LOW

No fault tolerance (no dead-time protection)

❗ Incorrect timing or logic → shoot-through → hardware damage
❗ Misconfiguration may result in motor "locking" (both MOSFETs active)
